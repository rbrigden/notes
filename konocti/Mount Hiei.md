Mount Hiei is ==a sacred 848-meter-high mountain located on the border between Kyoto and Shiga Prefectures in Japan==, famous for housing the sprawling [Enryaku-ji Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hiei) complex.
